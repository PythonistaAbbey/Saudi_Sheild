How a warning flows

Nothing reaches the public without a signature and three people

1 · Issuer

Authorised authority signs a CAP-style message

2 · Verify

Key and signature checked; failures quarantined

3 · Operator

Prepares; reviews AI drafts and checks

4 · Approver

A different person approves

5 · Publisher

Duty officer publishes after confirmation

6 · App

Verifies again with a pinned key, then shows it

In parallel and unchanged: the official cell broadcast and sirens. Saudi Shield adds context, history, languages and accessibility after them.

Updates and the “White” all-clear follow the same path and must reference the original alert. Every step is written to a tamper-evident audit log.

Architecture: docs/02-architecture.md · Demonstrated end to end in the simulator

Walk the chain left to right. The key point is separation of duties: the person who prepares cannot approve, and the publisher must be different again. Push notifications only wake the app; the content is always fetched and verified, so a spoofed notification cannot show a fake alert.
