Honest limits

What the prototype is not yet

Not connected to any official feed

All alerts are fictional exercises with demo keys. Live use needs an authorised feed and keys issued by the authority.

Demo security

Sign-in, PINs and an in-memory store are for demonstration. Production needs national SSO, an HSM and accredited hosting.

Simulated delivery

Delivery numbers are generated. No native builds, push providers or load tests yet.

Two languages, unreviewed

Arabic and English templates need review by the authority; other languages need certified translation.

None of this changes the core finding: the safety model of signatures, separation of duties and template-only public text works end to end.

Full list of limitations: docs/06-testing.md and docs/04-roadmap.md

Say this plainly before anyone asks. The prototype proves the workflow and the safeguards; it does not prove scale, real-world reliability or user acceptance. Those are what the pilot stages are for.
