Approvals required

Nothing goes live without these

Mandate

Ministry of Interior and the General Directorate of Civil Defense agree the role, scope and governance.

Authorised feed and keys

An official CAP-style feed and signing keys issued and managed by the authority.

Personal data

Compliance with the Personal Data Protection Law and SDAIA guidance, with an impact assessment.

Cybersecurity

NCA Essential and Cloud Cybersecurity Controls; accredited hosting in the Kingdom.

Telecom and app stores

CST alignment on notifications and messaging; store listings that make the app's status clear.

Name, identity and language

Naming and branding agreed with the authority and DGA; translations certified and approved.

Legal and operational dependencies: docs/01-research.md

The name Saudi Shield is a working title. It may be unsuitable for public use and should be agreed with the authority. No official logos are used anywhere in the prototype.
