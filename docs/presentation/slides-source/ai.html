AI assistance

AI assists. People decide.

What AI may do

Suggest a summary, flag unclear or conflicting wording, and translate short notes from an approved phrasebook. Every suggestion is labelled as a draft.

What AI may not do

Declare an emergency, choose a level or area, issue instructions or evacuation orders, or estimate anything about a threat.

Checks that block approval

Speculative words such as “possibly”, shelter and evacuate in one alert, numbers that disagree between languages, more than five steps.

The prototype uses a rule-based assistant behind a provider interface; a model could be swapped in under the same guardrails.

Public text is always rendered from approved templates and structured codes, never written freely by AI. That is how we avoid turning uncertain estimates into official-sounding instructions. The checker runs on every draft and its blocking findings must be resolved by a person.
